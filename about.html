Our Story & Heritage

Who We Are

As our name suggests, the Christian Reformed Church grew out of the 16th-century Protestant Reformation, instigated by Martin Luther on 31 October 1517 and quickly built upon by reformers such as Zwingli, Knox, Cranmer, and Calvin. Their faith became known as “The Reformed faith”, with its teachings grounded in the Scriptures of the Old and New Testaments, which is the Word of God.

Our local church fellowship started in 1954. We are part of a group of churches called the Christian Reformed Churches of Australia (CRCA). This denomination was born in the 1950s and traces its roots back to the Protestant Reformation, hence our name “Reformed”.

We belong to a worldwide community of Christian churches which developed from the Reformed and Presbyterian branches of the Reformation. Today the Christian Reformed Churches of Australia include over 50 congregations around the country, operating the Reformed Theological College in Geelong as well as retirement villages across Australia.

Church Government

Our Church Order

Scripture identifies Jesus Christ as the Head, the Shepherd, the Bridegroom, and the King of the Church. Following the biblical principle that “everything should be done in a fitting and orderly way” (1 Corinthians 14:40), our congregation functions under the Church Order of the Christian Reformed Churches of Australia (CRCA).

The Church Order was declared definitive in 1979 and thoughtfully updated through successive Synods (most notably ratified in 2003 when the denomination became known as the CRCA). Local elders and deacons serve together in pastoral care, biblical oversight, and practical ministry, while partnering with sister churches regionally (Classis) and nationally (Synod).
