Missions We Support

Living out our love for the lost and vulnerable across the Northern Beaches, local schools, Australian prisons, and the nations.

Local Community Partnership

One Meal – Northern Beaches

One of the five loves that defines our church is a love for the lost and vulnerable. It is often hard to know where to start, and thankfully local partners like One Meal have stepped in to help those in need across our community.

How you can take part on Sunday: Each week we collect non-perishable foods to donate to One Meal. Keep your eye out for the large collection basket right at the church entrance.

Local, National & Global

Our Mission Partners

Alongside weekly local care, our congregation partners with school Scripture teachers, prison mentors, anti-slavery advocates, and CRCA mission workers abroad.

Local & Pacific Outreach

Local Schools & Solomon Islands

Our local congregation actively supports Scripture Education at Dee Why Primary School and Cromer High School, sharing the Bible with students in our own neighbourhood.

Abroad, we place a particular emphasis on supporting our denomination’s missionary work in the Solomon Islands.

Australia & South Pacific

Crossroads Prison Ministries

Crossroads Prison Ministries assists in the rehabilitation of prisoners by bringing God’s Word behind prison walls through ongoing Correspondence Bible Study Courses.

More than 500 active inmate students and over 400 volunteer mentors across Australia connect in Christ-centred mentorship, sending encouragement and hope with every reviewed lesson. As a faith-based organisation without government funding, Crossroads relies wholly on prayer and donor generosity.

Visit cpm.org.au →
Global Justice

International Justice Mission (IJM)

International Justice Mission is the world’s largest anti-slavery organisation. Since 1997, IJM has rescued over 76,000 people from slavery, provided trauma-informed aftercare, sought prosecution of perpetrators, and partnered with governments and law enforcement across 24 countries.

Responding to Isaiah 1:17 (“seek justice and defend the oppressed”), the CRCA has supported IJM through recommended offerings since 2015.

Visit ijm.org.au →
